      About times and ranges
      • History keeps the last 30 days of readings. Older readings move to Archives automatically.
      • Times are shown in your browser’s time zone, with dates as DD/MM/YYYY.
      • est. marks an estimated time: the monitor’s clock had not been set yet (for example just after a restart), so the time was worked out from how long it had been running. It may be off by a few seconds.
      • A custom range can cover up to 31 days. CSV exports contain every reading in the range, oldest first, split into files of up to 50,000 readings.
      • Gas response is the MQ-135 sensor’s relative response in millivolts, not an ammonia (ppm) measurement. Ratio compares it with the calibration baseline.

        Gas warning threshold

        10–2399 mV, measured at the board’s divided sensor input (the same scale as the gas readings). This is a relative gas response, not ammonia ppm.

        Timing

        1–10. Higher values ignore short spikes but warn later.

        2–60 s. Calibration always measures every 2 s, then returns to this.

        Warnings stay paused while the sensor warms up after a restart, during calibration, and while a sensor reports an error, whatever these settings are.

        Saving stores the settings you want. The device picks them up on its next check-in and then confirms them above.

Proposal: not the active build, not tested on the C3.
  • MQ-135 and I2C wiring are unchanged, so MQ-135 readings keep the same ADC path as previous runs.
  • The SD pins (CS 10, SCK 6, MOSI 7, MISO 1) are the same C3 SD mapping as the earlier sd_card_reader_test sketch.
  • Leave GPIO2, 8, 9 (boot/strapping; 8 = onboard LED, 9 = BOOT button) and GPIO20/21 (UART) off the SD card. GPIO0 stays free.
  • Adapter B (the one that passed the bench tests) has no regulator: its 3V3 pin feeds the card directly, so it goes to the C3's 3V3, never 5V.
  • The card then shares the C3's small 3.3 V regulator with Wi-Fi, the AHT/BMP and the ADC. Add a 100 µF capacitor (10 µF minimum) across the adapter's 3V3 and GND, close to the adapter. During testing, MQ mV around SD writes is compared with quiet periods to confirm the readings are not disturbed.
  • Pin order on the header differs between boards and modules. Go by the silkscreen labels.

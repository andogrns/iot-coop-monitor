Power the adapter from 5V/VIN, not 3V3. Its onboard AMS1117-3.3 regulator needs about 5 V in; the 74LVC125A buffer already gives 3.3 V logic, so the signal pins wire straight to the ESP32. Pin positions shown are the common 30-pin DevKit V1 header; always go by the silkscreen labels on your board.
